LITTLE MOMENTS, BIG MEMORIES

Our Storybook 💗

Tap each chapter. Some memories are photographs; some are moments we never want to forget.

Before you begin... 🌷

Maybe some of these moments were ordinary days for you. But for me, they became memories worth keeping. We never planned to create them. They just happened — between lectures, practicals, jokes and random conversations.

So, let's go back for a moment...

🌱 01 — The beginning

When our college story started...

Tap to open ↓

When college began, everything was new: faces, classrooms, people and possibilities. Initially, it was me and Sneha who had started becoming friends. Then, a few days into college, you became part of our little circle.

I don't think any of us knew that those ordinary introductions would turn into so many stories, jokes, secrets and memories.

Sometimes the best friendships begin without a plan.

📱 02 — From Snapchat to WhatsApp

A little compliment, a new connection.

Tap to open ↓

Our conversations started on Snapchat around the beginning of October. Somewhere in those early chats, I told you that I liked your eyes and your voice. Just a small compliment, but it became one of those little details from the beginning that I still remember.

Then, around my birthday in October, you messaged me on WhatsApp, and our conversations continued there too. From Snapchat chats to WhatsApp messages — one more little chapter in how our friendship grew.

One of the little things I told you I liked. 💗
Sometimes a simple conversation becomes the start of a lovely memory.

👭 03 — Our little college gang

Different personalities, one collection of memories.

Tap to open ↓

There was Sneha, Altaf, you and me — each with our own personality and our own way of creating chaos. It wasn't about doing something extraordinary every day. Sometimes just being together was enough.

I wish we could pause some of those ordinary college days and look at them properly. We probably didn't realise how memorable they would become.

Our little group had its own kind of magic.

🎨 04 — 8 October 2023

The model-making competition.

Tap to open ↓

I remember the model-making competition. I was there with Sneha and Altaf, and you helped with the drawing and setting everything up.

There was work to do, things to arrange and ideas to put together. What makes me remember that day isn't just the competition. It's that we were making memories without even knowing it.

A little teamwork, a little chaos, and a memory that stayed.

🩹 05 — The HAP practical

"Don't worry, just look at me."

Tap to open ↓

You were scared of the finger-prick. I remember telling you, "Don't worry, just look at me. Let me do it for you."

I took your hand, and you held my forearm tightly. It was such a small practical moment. For everyone else, it might have been just another activity. But I remember the way you trusted me enough to feel a little less nervous.

One tiny practical. One memory I never forgot.

🤝 06 — The first-semester viva

A nervous moment, a little reassurance.

Tap to open ↓

January. First-semester practicals and viva. I remember the common room, the nervous atmosphere and you holding my hand. We stayed that way for more than half an hour.

There wasn't any big conversation or dramatic scene. Just two friends, one nervous moment and a little comfort in being there for each other.

Sometimes a little hand to hold makes a difficult moment easier.

🫶 07 — A moment captured

Some pictures bring back more than memories.

Tap to open ↓

This photograph has its own little place in my collection. It's one of those separate moments when a simple picture captured something I still remember.

One photograph. So many things to remember.

🍟 08 — Canteen, lunch and off-class moments

The ordinary days that never felt ordinary.

Tap to open ↓

The canteen visits, lunch breaks, sitting together, off-class moments and random conversations. Sometimes we had absolutely nothing important to do, but somehow there was always something to talk about.

Maybe that's what makes these memories special. They weren't extraordinary while they were happening. They became special when I started looking back.

Ordinary days. Unforgettable company.

🎶 09 — Random songs and endless conversations

One topic leading to another, as always.

Tap to open ↓

Our conversations could go in literally any direction. One minute we would be talking about something serious, and the next minute the topic would be completely random.

And then there were the songs. Random songs, references and conversations that somehow continued even when there was no particular reason to keep talking.

We didn't need interesting topics. We made random topics interesting.

🔐 10 — The things we shared

Little secrets, trust and understanding.

Tap to open ↓

There were conversations that weren't meant for everyone. Little things we shared, thoughts we expressed, and moments when we could talk freely.

Trust grows through small things: listening, understanding, keeping someone's words safe and being there without making everything complicated.

Some memories are not photographs. They are things only two friends understand.

😂 11 — Our fights, teasing and making up

Because we were never serious for too long.

Tap to open ↓

We weren't always sweet and understanding. There were disagreements, teasing, silly arguments and moments when one of us got annoyed with the other.

But then things would become normal again, and we'd go back to talking, laughing and being ourselves. Not every moment has to be perfect. What matters is the care underneath all the madness.

A little fighting, a little teasing, and a lot of memories.

🌙 12 — Your wonderfully weird stories

Some conversations were simply unbelievable!

Tap to open ↓

How can I forget your dream stories? Sometimes you'd tell me something so unexpected that I genuinely wouldn't know what to say first.

The stories, explanations, random details and my reactions — those conversations had their own entertainment value.

Never stop telling your weird stories. They're part of your charm!

💗 13 — If I could revisit one thing...

I'd choose the ordinary days.

Tap to open ↓

Would it be the model-making competition? The HAP practical? The nervous first-semester viva? The canteen conversations? Or those random moments when we laughed at absolutely nothing?

Maybe I wouldn't choose just one. I'd choose a normal college day — a day when nobody knew it would become a memory. A day when we were simply being ourselves.

If I could revisit those days, I'd happily live those little moments all over again.
🐼💗

One last thing, Sakshi...

I don't know how many of these moments you remember. Maybe some will make you smile, some will make you laugh, and maybe some will make you say, "Do you seriously remember that?" 😂

But I do remember. Not because every moment was extraordinary, but because you were part of them.

Some people become memories. Some become a part of the story. 💗
